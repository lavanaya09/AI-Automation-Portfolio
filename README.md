  # AI Customer Inquiry Automation
  An AI-powered customer support automation built with Make.com, Google Forms, OpenAI, and Gmail.

  ## What it does
  
  This workflow automatically:
  - Captures customer inquiries through a Google Form
  - Processes the submitted information with OpenAI
  - Generates personalized AI response based on the inquiry of the customer
  - Sends the response automatically to the customer's email

  ##Tools Used

  - Make.com - Workflow Automation
  - Google Forms - Customer inquiry collection
  - Open AI - AI response generation
  - Gmail - Automated email delivery

  ##Use Case

  This automation can help online businesses reduce manual customer-support work by automatically responding to common inquiries such as order issues, missing items, returns, exchanges, and product questions.

  ##Workflow

  Google Forms - OpenAI - Gmail

  ##Project Outcome

  The system was tested with different customer inquiries and successfully generated AI responses based on the submitted information
